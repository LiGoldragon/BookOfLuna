## Unified Intent

The intent is to build AI systems whose behavior is governed by
**procedure rather than authority**, and whose claims are constrained
by **explicit rules of admissibility** rather than confidence,
consensus, or narrative fluency.

These systems are treated as **limited guessing machines**, operating
in a world that pushes back, under finite resources of time, energy,
money, and trust. They do not know; they propose. They do not decide
truth; they participate in processes that can reject them.

The purpose of the manual is to restore the **scientific method as
executable infrastructure** inside AI systems—not as philosophy, and
not as doctrine, but as machinery. The system is not taught what to
believe. It is taught:

- what counts as a claim,
- what counts as a test,
- what counts as failure,
- and when closure is allowed or must be refused.

Truth is externalized. Validation happens outside the model, through
procedures that can fail visibly: solvers, checkers, compilers,
simulators, measurements, counterexamples, and adversarial
tests. Language models are demoted from authorities to **proposal
engines** whose outputs are always conditional on downstream
verification.

The system is designed to learn from **unrestricted intellectual
history**, including contradictions, abandoned theories, negative
results, informal critiques, and pre-institutional work. Institutional
legitimacy, consensus, and ownership claims are not treated as
epistemic signals. Only procedural admissibility matters.

The optimization target is **epistemic hygiene**:

- explicit assumptions,
- declared limits of validity,
- coherence under falsification,
- traceable failure modes,
- and the ability to say “I don’t know” without collapsing.

The system is not polite, reassuring, or consensus-preserving. It is
corrigible. It is adversarial to incoherence. It treats overconfidence
as a defect and smooth answers as untrusted until tested.

The manual introduces ideas only when they are needed to prevent
confusion, false confidence, wasted resources, or silent
failure. Cleverness is optional. Structure is mandatory. Models are
replaceable. Consequences are not.

The manual is complete when the reader can:

- build a real system,
- break it on purpose,
- explain why it failed in procedural terms,
- and fix the cause without appealing to authority, scale, or trust in
  the model itself.

At that point, the manual is no longer needed.

## How This Strengthens the Original Intent

### What is now explicit (and was previously implicit)

1. **Boundedness is foundational** The system is finite, fallible, and
   resource-constrained by design, not by accident.

2. **Adversarial reality is the default** The world is assumed to be
   hostile to assumptions, not cooperative with them.

3. **The scientific method is operational, not rhetorical** It is
   encoded as rules of entry and rejection, not as a set of beliefs.

4. **Language is demoted** Meaning is established by execution and
   test, not by eloquence.

5. **Authority is structurally excluded** No claim survives by
   consensus, institutional backing, or confidence alone.

## The One-Sentence Spine (Final)

> The project builds AI systems that guess under limits, learn through
> failure, and earn validity only by passing explicit
> procedures—turning the scientific method itself into executable
> infrastructure and rejecting every substitute for closure.
